Mr. Anjani S. Kedia

Director

Mr. Anjani S. Kedia is a co-founder and Director of Novel Knitts Private Limited, joining the company in 2026. A Commerce Graduate and BBA holder, he brings over 24 years of industry experience rooted in a family legacy of textile business spanning three generations. He oversees Finance, Design, and Quality at Novel Knitts, bringing deep domain expertise and a strong entrepreneurial vision to the company.
