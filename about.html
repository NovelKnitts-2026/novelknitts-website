Our Story

Where Thread Becomes Artistry

Founded in 2022 and rebranded in 2026, headquartered in Surat, Gujarat, Novel Knitts Private Limited is a dynamic textile manufacturing and trading company specialising in premium crochet fabrics for the garment industry. In April 2026, the company underwent a strategic rebranding as Novel Knitts Private Limited, reflecting its renewed vision, growth, and commitment to excellence.

Established by Mr. Anjani Kedia and Mr. Divyansh Marodia, the company is dedicated to delivering innovative, high-quality fabric solutions that meet the evolving needs of fashion brands, garment manufacturers, and textile businesses across India and international markets.

With an in-house manufacturing setup spread across 15,000 square feet, equipped with advanced crochet machines and a production capacity of approximately 50,000 metres, we combine craftsmanship, technology, and industry expertise to create fabrics that meet the highest standards of quality and design.

At Novel Knitts, quality is our top priority. Every fabric undergoes a thorough quality inspection process, and only approved materials are delivered to our customers. Our dedicated team of 30+ professionals works with a customer-centric approach to ensure reliability, consistency, and timely delivery.
