Who We Are

Company Overview

Novel Knitts Private Limited is a leading textile manufacturing and trading company based in Surat, Gujarat, specialising in premium crochet fabrics for the garment industry. Originally founded in 2022, the company embarked on a transformative journey and in April 2026, relaunched under the brand identity of Novel Knitts Private Limited — marking a new chapter of growth, ambition, and elevated standards.

Co-founded by Mr. Anjani Kedia and Mr. Divyansh Marodia, Novel Knitts is built on a foundation of innovation and reliability, with a clear focus on delivering high-quality fabric solutions to fashion brands, garment manufacturers, and textile businesses across India and global markets.
